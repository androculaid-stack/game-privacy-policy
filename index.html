1. Introduction

Welcome to the mobile games published by Androculaid. We respect your privacy and aim to provide an enjoyable gaming experience. Our games are designed primarily for offline gameplay. We do not operate our own servers to collect or store players' personal information.

This policy applies to the mobile games published by Androculaid that link to this Privacy Policy.

2. Offline Gameplay and Data Storage

  • Our games do not require an account or registration for offline gameplay.
  • We do not operate our own user-data servers.
  • We do not upload players' gameplay information or personal information to our own servers.
  • Game progress, preferences, scores and settings may be stored locally on your device, where applicable.
  • We do not maintain our own central database of players.

Locally stored information may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

3. Advertising - Google AdMob

Our games may use Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements that allow players to receive in-game rewards.

Loading and displaying advertisements requires an internet connection, even though the main gameplay is designed to work offline.

Google AdMob may collect or process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers where available, and advertising interaction information. Google handles this information according to its own privacy policies and applicable settings.

We do not independently store this advertising information on our own servers.
